# /platform-checkup

Description: A month-by-month health check for one ad platform, with the weakest month flagged.

## How to use it
Type `/platform-checkup` and a platform name, e.g. `/platform-checkup TikTok`.
If no platform is given, check Meta.

## What to do
1. For the chosen platform, get these numbers for each month of 2025:
   spend, clicks, conversions, CTR, CPA and ROAS.
2. Show one row per month, oldest first, with a column named `month`.

## How to write the answer
- Start with one sentence: is this platform healthy overall, judged by ROAS?
- Name the best month and the weakest month by ROAS, with their numbers.
- Point out any month where CPA jumped by more than 25% from the month before.
- End with one suggestion for what the marketing team should look into next.
